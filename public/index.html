Responsible Soil and Rock Mining

Unregulated extraction leads to erosion, habitat loss, and unsafe working conditions. EarthLink brings transparency and accountability by connecting officials, dealers, and communities on a single platform.

Problems We Address

Illegal mining, stock hoarding, and opaque supply chains reduce trust and harm the environment. Citizens struggle to report issues, and officials lack real-time visibility.

How EarthLink Works

Dealers apply with licenses for approval by officials. Approved dealers upload mined quantities. Customers can request purchases from verified listings. Citizens can report illegal activities with photos and location, which officials review and act upon.

Outcomes

Traceable inventory, faster enforcement against illegal mining, and a safer, more sustainable marketplace for soil and rock.
